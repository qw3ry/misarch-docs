# Business Processes

In the following, the most important business processes are modeled using BPMN.

:::note

These models describe the intended business processes, not the way the services coordinate them at runtime.
The buy process below shows its steps in parallel with a joining gateway, and compensation handled centrally.
The implementation is event-based and essentially linear, and compensation is triggered by the service that detected the failure.
See [Order Saga and Failure Model](/architecture/checkout) for how the buy process is actually coordinated.

:::
